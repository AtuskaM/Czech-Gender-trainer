Rod + vzor + proč / Gender + model + why

How to play

For each word, answer all three questions: choose its gender, choose its model, and choose one simple reason that explains both. Then press Check. Nothing is marked until you have answered all three.

Easy shows an adjective with the noun and its English meaning. Normal shows only the noun. You can switch modes during a level. Shuffle mixes the words you have not finished; Skip moves past a word without scoring it.
